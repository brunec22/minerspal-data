Privacy Policy for MinersPal

Effective date: 23 November 2026

This policy explains what data the MinersPal app for iPhone and iPad ("MinersPal", "the app") handles, why, and what choices you have.

Who we are

MinersPal is published by AB DIGITECH, Alen Brunec s.p. (Slovenia), the data controller for the data described here. Contact: info@minerspal.com.

Data that stays on your device

  • Miners and pools you add: miner name, coin, pool, public wallet address or pool username, and the statistics loaded for them (hashrate, workers, balances, payouts, history).
  • Pool API keys for pools that need them (for example NiceHash, Braiins Pool, AntPool, ViaBTC, Foundry USA, Luxor). They are kept in the iOS Keychain on your device and are only sent to the pool they belong to.
  • Portfolio, favourite coins, smart alerts, farm settings (such as power draw and electricity price) and app settings.

We do not receive this data. Backups you export from the app are files you control.

Services the app contacts

To show your stats, prices and news, the app sends requests directly from your device to:

  • the mining pools you add (with your public wallet address, pool username or API key, as the pool requires);
  • public market and data sources for coin prices, exchange rates and mining profitability (for example crypto.com, BitPay exchange rates and WhatToMine);
  • our public data files hosted on GitHub Pages (news, reward estimates, hardware lists).

These services receive standard request data such as your IP address and are governed by their own privacy policies. We do not send them your name or email address.

Optional community account

The community features are optional. If you sign in (Sign in with Apple, Google Sign-In or email and password), we use Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore) to store:

  • your account identifier and the email address provided by the sign-in method;
  • your chosen username and account dates;
  • the posts, replies and reports you write, with the app version, device type and a random app installation identifier, used for moderation and abuse prevention;
  • your miners (name, coin, pool, public wallet address or pool username). If "public miners" is turned on in your community profile, other community users can see them. You can turn this off at any time.

Content you post in the community is visible to other users.

Notifications

Smart alerts (hashrate, offline workers, payouts, prices) are checked on your device and shown as local notifications. If you allow notifications, the app also registers the device with Apple Push Notification service and Firebase Cloud Messaging so we can deliver notifications; this uses a device push token, not your identity.

Purchases

MinersPal Max and MinersPal+ are sold through Apple's App Store. Apple processes the payment; we never see your payment details. The app checks your purchase and subscription status with Apple's StoreKit.

Support emails

If you contact support from the app, your mail app opens an email to info@minerspal.com that includes the app version, device type, iOS version, currency and your miners' public wallet addresses, pools and usernames to help us find the problem. You can edit or remove any of this before sending. We use support emails only to answer you.

No tracking, no ads, no analytics

MinersPal does not show ads, does not use advertising identifiers, does not track you across other apps or websites, and does not sell your data. App analytics are turned off.

Legal basis and retention

We process account and community data to provide the features you choose to use (performance of a contract) and to keep the community safe (legitimate interest). Support emails are kept as long as needed to handle your request. Community data is kept until you delete your account or ask us to delete it.

Your rights and choices

  • Delete local data at any time by removing miners in the app or deleting the app.
  • Delete your community account from the Community screen in the app. For full erasure of your account data, email us.
  • Under the GDPR you may request access, correction, erasure, restriction or a copy of your data, and object to processing, by writing to info@minerspal.com. You may also complain to your data protection authority (in Slovenia: Informacijski pooblaščenec, www.ip-rs.si).

Children

MinersPal is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country), and we do not knowingly collect their data.

International transfers

Firebase services are provided by Google and may process data outside the EU under Google's standard contractual safeguards.

Changes

We may update this policy. The current version is always at this address, with its effective date above.
